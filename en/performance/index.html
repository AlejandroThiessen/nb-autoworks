02/Why here

Service and strip, under one roof

Most performance shops don't service anything, and most service shops don't build anything fast. We do both, in the same building and with the same people.

That matters more than it sounds. A supercharged engine needs different maintenance, and a fault on a modified vehicle isn't diagnosed the same way. If we built it, we also know how it behaves afterwards.

And what we sell, we race. If something doesn't hold up, we find out first.

03/What we do

What we do

Six fronts. Hardly any build touches only one of them.

  • Superchargers

    A full install, not just bolting the kit on. We check that the fuel system, cooling and exhaust can handle what you're about to ask of it, and calibrate from there.

  • Turbo

    Turbo kits, intercooling and the supporting work that comes with them. We tell you up front what else it will need, because a turbo on its own is almost never a turbo on its own.

  • E85 and ethanol

    Ethanol conversion and the fuel system upgrades it requires. E85 makes power and cools the chamber, but it demands more flow: pump, injectors and lines.

  • Tuning and ECU

    Calibration for what you're actually running. Datalogging, fuel and timing adjustment, and a check after the first few runs. A kit without a tune is a half-finished kit.

  • Exhaust and intake

    Headers, exhaust and intake, chosen for the engine rather than the noise. We'll tell you what actually gains and what only sounds like it does.

  • Drag racing

    Quarter-mile prep: suspension, traction, differential and everything that breaks once the rest of it holds.

04/Parts

Go fast parts

We source what you need. If you already know what you want, give us the brand and part number. If not, we will tell you which combination makes sense for your engine and your budget.

05/The process

How a build works

  1. We talk

    What you want from the vehicle, what you use it for and how far you want to take it.

  2. Plan and quote

    We put together the full list, not just the headline part. No surprises halfway through.

  3. Parts

    We source what was agreed and give you timings before we start.

  4. Build

    Measured, documented work. We send you progress as it goes.

  5. Calibration

    It's tuned for what you're running and for the fuel you'll actually use.

  6. Validation

    Testing, leak checks and a re-check after the first few runs.

Tell us what you have in mind

01

Preventive Maintenance

The service that keeps the big bills away. Oil and filter at the interval your vehicle actually calls for, plus fluids, belts, battery and brakes. We tell you what's fine and what will need attention soon, without selling you what you don't need.

What's included

  • Oil and filter change
  • All fluid levels checked
  • Air and cabin filters
  • Serpentine belt and hoses

02

Brakes

Pads, rotors, fluid and ABS. We resurface rotors in-house on our own lathe, so you're not waiting on an outside machine shop. If the pedal feels off or there's a noise when you brake, bring it in before it gets expensive.

What's included

  • Front and rear pads
  • Rotor resurfacing or replacement
  • Brake fluid change
  • Calipers and lines checked

03

Suspension & Steering

Shocks, springs, bushings, ball joints and tie rods. Also lift kits and levelling for trucks, with the alignment afterwards, which is where most shops stop short. If it bounces, clunks, or wears a tyre on one edge, we will find it.

What's included

  • Shocks and springs
  • Bushings, ball joints and tie rods
  • Lift kits and levelling
  • Alignment after the work
Learn More — Suspension & Steering

04

Diagnostics & Repair

Factory-level scan tools, not a generic code reader. We pull codes, watch live data and run real tests before replacing anything. Finding the fault is the job; swapping the part is the easy bit.

What's included

  • Full module scan
  • Check-engine and dash lights
  • Electrical and no-start faults
  • Sensors and wiring
Learn More — Diagnostics & Repair

Engine & Transmission

Timing chains, gaskets, heads, clutch and transmission. Full engine work when it's needed, with tolerances measured and recorded. It's the most expensive job there is, so we do it once.

What's included

  • Timing chains and belts
  • Gaskets and cylinder heads
  • Engine rebuilds
  • Clutch and flywheel
Learn More — Engine & Transmission

Tires, Balancing & Alignment

Mounting, laser balancing and truck alignment. Vibration at highway speed is almost always balance; uneven wear is almost always alignment. Both get sorted here.

    Common questions

    Before you book

    Book Appointment (opens in a new tab)
    Do estimates cost anything?

    No. We inspect, tell you what we found, and you decide.

    Do you use original parts?

    OEM or trusted brands. If you'd rather use something else, we'll explain the trade-offs first.

    How often should I change the oil?

    It depends on the vehicle, the oil type and how you drive it. On your first visit we check what your model calls for and confirm the right interval.

    How do I know if I need brakes?

    Squealing when you brake, a pedal that travels too far, vibration under braking, or the wheel pulling to one side. Any of those, bring it in.

    When do I need an alignment?

    After new tyres or suspension work, if the wheel sits off-centre, or if a tyre is wearing on one edge only.

    How long does a service take?

    Routine maintenance is usually same-day. Engine and transmission work depends on the job, and we give you an estimated time before we start.

Services

Preventive Maintenance

Oil, filters, fluids and mileage-based service. What keeps the big bills away.

Preventive maintenance is the cheapest thing you will ever pay for on a vehicle, and the thing that saves you the most. An oil change on time costs a fraction of an engine.

We do not work off a generic interval. We check what your model calls for, how you drive it and the conditions you drive in, and the plan comes from that. If something still has life in it we will say so; we do not replace parts to pad the bill.

What's included

What the service covers

  • Oil and filter change
  • All fluid levels checked
  • Air and cabin filters
  • Serpentine belt and hoses
  • Battery and alternator test
  • Brake and tyre inspection

Warning signs

How to tell it is due

If you notice any of these, do not leave it.

  • You are past the mileage from your last service

  • The oil looks black or smells burnt

  • Hard cold starts, or the engine sounds rough

  • The service light came on

The process

How we do it

  1. 01

    We inspect

    We check oil, fluids, filters, belt, battery, brakes and tyres. All of it documented with photos.

  2. 02

    We report

    We send you what we found, split into what is urgent and what can wait. You decide what gets done.

  3. 03

    We do it

    Service with name-brand parts, and we note down when the next one is due.

Questions

About this service

Synthetic or conventional oil?

It depends on what your engine calls for. If your vehicle is designed for synthetic, running conventional does it harm. We will tell you which one it takes.

How many kilometres between services?

It varies by model and by use. We check what your vehicle calls for and confirm the interval on your first visit.

Will you tell me if you find something else?

Always. We send photos of what we found and you approve before we do anything extra.

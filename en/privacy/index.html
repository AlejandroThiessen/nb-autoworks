Privacy notice

How we handle the personal data you share with us.

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Who is responsible for your data

NB Autoworks, at C. Monte 412, 31607 Campo 4, Chihuahua, Mexico, is responsible for the use and protection of the personal data you provide to us. This notice explains how.

What data we collect

We collect only what you give us directly: your name, phone number, email address if you choose to share it, details of your vehicle, and the message you write to us. We do not collect sensitive personal data.

What we use it for

We use your personal data for the following purposes, all of which are necessary to provide the service you asked for:

  • Contacting you to answer your enquiry or estimate.
  • Booking, confirming and following up on your appointment.
  • Keeping the service history of your vehicle.
  • Reminding you when your next service is due.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share it only with providers that help us operate, such as the system we use to book appointments, and only for the purposes described above. We may also share it where a competent authority requires it under the law.

Your ARCO rights

You have the right to know what personal data we hold about you and what we use it for (Access), to ask us to correct anything inaccurate (Rectification), to ask us to delete data you consider unnecessary (Cancellation), and to object to specific uses (Opposition). You may also withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us by phone or come to the shop. We will ask for identification to confirm it is you, and we will respond within the timeframes set by law.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will publish the updated version on this page with its new date. We recommend checking it from time to time.
